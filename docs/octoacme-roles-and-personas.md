# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality strategy and ensure acceptance criteria are met before release. They collaborate with developers and product managers to define testable requirements and prevent defects from reaching production.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain test strategy and test plans
- Create and maintain acceptance criteria frameworks
- Execute manual and coordinate automated testing
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigations
- Participate in requirement reviews and Definition of Done (DoD) refinement
- Report on quality metrics and test coverage
- Validate that all acceptance criteria are met before release

### Goals
- Ensure software meets acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Reduce escaped defects reaching production
- Enable fast, confident releases through comprehensive testing
- Drive continuous improvement in quality practices

### Typical Communication
- Daily standup participation and QA status updates
- Test planning and requirement review meetings
- Quality metrics dashboards and release readiness reports
- Collaboration with developers on test-driven development
- Integration with the Quality & Testing section of Execution & Tracking processes

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical direction and architectural guidance for projects. They ensure technical decisions align with system goals, mentor developers, and help manage technical risks and dependencies.

### Responsibilities
- Review and approve technical designs and architecture
- Mentor developers on best practices and patterns
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigations
- Manage technical dependencies and integrations with other teams
- Participate in design reviews and code reviews
- Assist in effort estimation for technical complexity
- Help define technical acceptance criteria

### Goals
- Ensure technically sound, scalable, and maintainable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and maintenance burden
- Enable faster delivery through good design decisions
- Support team skill development and knowledge sharing

### Typical Communication
- Technical design review meetings
- Code review comments and architecture discussions
- Architecture documentation and decision logs (ADRs)
- Mentoring sessions and technical guidance
- Cross-team dependency coordination

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile processes, remove blockers, and coach teams on continuous improvement. They enable the team to self-organize and deliver value incrementally while supporting the team rhythm defined in Execution & Tracking.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate daily standups, sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives
- Remove impediments and escalate blockers to appropriate parties
- Coach team on agile practices and principles
- Maintain project board and sprint health
- Track team velocity and identify process improvements
- Shield team from external distractions and context switching
- Support Definition of Done (DoD) enforcement and sprint commitments

### Goals
- Enable consistent team velocity and predictability
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement and psychological safety
- Ensure effective sprint execution and team self-organization
- Minimize waste and optimize delivery cadence

### Typical Communication
- Facilitating daily standups and sprint ceremonies
- Impediment escalation and resolution tracking
- Retrospective facilitation and action item tracking
- Team coaching on agile best practices and process guidance
- Metrics and health reports on team performance

---

## Executive Sponsor / Stakeholder Lead

### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide business context, secure resources, and ensure alignment with organizational strategy. They represent business interests, make go/no-go decisions, and champion projects at the leadership level.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and scope (from Project Initiation phase)
- Secure budget and resource allocation
- Provide business context and define success metrics
- Make escalated decisions on scope and priority trade-offs
- Review milestone progress and provide business feedback
- Communicate business value and project status to the organization
- Support risk escalation and decision-making per the Risk Management escalation path

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver measurable business value
- Maintain stakeholder alignment and organizational confidence
- Enable informed decision-making on strategic trade-offs
- Minimize business disruption and maximize ROI

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and charter approval meetings
- Monthly stakeholder updates and milestone reviews
- Escalation and decision-making meetings for critical risks
- Roadmap and priority alignment sessions
- Executive visibility into project health and risks

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business needs and technical implementation. They gather and clarify requirements, define acceptance criteria, and validate that solutions meet business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct stakeholder interviews and gather business requirements
- Document and prioritize requirements with clear acceptance criteria
- Translate business needs into technical specifications
- Validate that solutions meet business objectives
- Participate in requirement reviews and DoD definition
- Identify gaps between stated and actual requirements
- Support user acceptance testing (UAT) and validation

### Goals
- Ensure requirements clarity and traceability
- Reduce rework caused by unclear or missed requirements
- Improve communication between business and technical teams
- Enable faster, more accurate solution delivery

### Typical Communication
- Requirements gathering sessions and stakeholder interviews
- Requirement documentation and acceptance criteria definition
- Collaboration with Product Managers and Developers on clarification
- User acceptance testing (UAT) coordination
- Change request and requirements impact analysis

## How these personas are used in the exercise

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Reference these roles when defining RACI matrices and responsibility assignments for projects.
- Use these personas to ensure all critical perspectives are represented in project planning and execution.
